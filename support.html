Common questions

How do I post a task in the Hub?

Open the Hub tab → tap + in the top-right → fill in title, description, category and budget → publish. Specialists can send proposals which appear in the task detail screen; you can accept one to open a private chat.

How do I respond to a task as a specialist?

In the Hub tab, tap any open task → tap Respond to task → write a short pitch → send. The author will see your response and may accept it, which opens a private chat between the two of you.

How do I report or block someone?

Inside any chat or user profile, tap the ⋯ menu in the top-right and choose Report (opens an email to our moderation team) or Block (hides their content from your view immediately). Reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

What is X5 Pro and how do I cancel?

X5 Pro is an optional auto-renewing monthly subscription priced at $9.99/month (or local-currency equivalent). It unlocks the full course library, priority placement in the Hub and an ad-free experience.

  • The subscription auto-renews unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period.
  • Manage or cancel anytime: iOS Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions → X5 Pro.
  • Apple's Standard EULA applies: https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: tap the profile icon → Settings (gear icon) → scroll to Danger zone → Delete account → confirm twice. Deletion is immediate and permanent: profile, portfolio, tasks and messages are removed.

Why does the app ask for the microphone?

Only when you record a voice message in a chat. The audio is sent only to the recipient of that chat. We never use the microphone in the background.

Why does the app ask for Photos?

Only when you attach a photo to a chat or upload a portfolio item. We don't read photos you don't pick.

Sign in with Apple, Google or email — which should I use?

Whichever you trust most. All three are supported. Apple's "Hide my email" option is honoured.
